Advisory & Applied-AI Practice

Building evidence systems for how organizations make decisions.

Greenbaum Labs is an independent advisory and applied-AI practice founded by Justin R. Greenbaum. It works on organizational diagnostics, operating-model design and enterprise AI adoption.

The practice develops Decision & Responsibility Infrastructure®: methods and tools for making the structural conditions beneath organizational performance observable. Its diagnostic method is Coherence, which shows leaders where truth, decision authority and continuity are breaking down and stalling progress.

The work centers on three questions

Truth

Vertex 01

Does the narrative match the evidence?

Authority

Vertex 02

Is it clear who can decide and who is accountable?

Continuity

Vertex 03

Do decisions, their reasons, and the organization’s learning persist over time?

The Instrument

The external diagnostic measures the gap between what an organization says and what the world experiences. Its pipeline combines public evidence, local inference, multi-model analysis, adversarial review, and reproducible reporting.

Model inference runs on owned infrastructure. Automation may retrieve, compare, challenge, and suggest. Judgment remains human.
