DRI Practice · Privacy

Privacy

Effective: October 6, 2026
Applies to: dripractice.com and its subpages

Who runs this site

DRI Practice is the work of Justin R. Greenbaum, run as a practice at Greenbaum Labs, LLC, an independent advisory and applied-AI practice based in New Jersey, United States. This page describes what happens to information about you when you use this site and its tools. The Labs site has its own privacy policy.

What the tools do with what you enter

The assessment and the seven instruments run entirely in your browser. Your answers, anything you paste into a tool, and the results they produce are processed on your device and are not sent to this site or to any server. There are no accounts and no forms.

Two things to know. The Declaration Log saves entries in your browser's local storage so they are still there when you come back; they stay on your device until you clear them in the tool or clear your browser data, and the tool can export them as a file you download. Several instruments let you export a result as a file; that file goes only to your downloads folder.

What this site collects

Server logs. The site is hosted on Vercel. When your browser requests a page, Vercel logs the IP address, browser type, referrer, and time, keeps those logs under its standard retention, and uses them for security and debugging.

Page-view analytics. Every page loads Vercel Web Analytics, which records each page view with the page address, the referrer, approximate location (country, region, city), browser, operating system, and device type. It sets no cookies. Visits are grouped under a hash of the request that Vercel discards after 24 hours, and the data is kept as aggregate counts that are not tied to an individual or an IP address. The Start Here page also records three button clicks (which door a visitor chose). These events record the click, never what you typed or answered.

Fonts and libraries. Most pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives the request (IP address and browser type) when your browser fetches a font file. The seven instruments use fonts served from this site. The Autoresearch page loads a charting library (Chart.js) from the jsDelivr content delivery network, which receives the same request data. Neither Google nor jsDelivr is under our control.

What this site does not collect

  • No accounts, logins, or passwords
  • No form submissions; contact is by email only
  • No payment information
  • No advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking
  • Nothing you enter into the assessment or the instruments

Third parties

  • Vercel: hosting, edge delivery, and page-view analytics
  • Google Fonts: font delivery
  • jsDelivr: a charting library on the Autoresearch page
  • Substack: the subscribe and "Read on Substack" links take you to Substack, which handles anything you do there, including a subscription, under its own policy. Nothing from Substack is embedded on this site.

Links to LinkedIn, the Labs site, and the personal site take you to properties with their own policies.

Email

If you email justin@greenbaumlabs.com, the message is kept as ordinary correspondence and used only to reply to you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, applicable law may give you rights regarding personal information we hold about you. If you want to exercise one, email justin@greenbaumlabs.com and we will respond.

Two practical limits. Assessment answers and tool inputs are processed on your device and never reach us, so there is nothing of that kind for us to retrieve or delete. Analytics is held only as aggregate counts, so it cannot be traced back to you by us either. What we can act on is email correspondence and anything you have sent us directly.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, email the address above and we will delete it.

Changes

If this page changes, the effective date above will change with it and the change will be described here.
