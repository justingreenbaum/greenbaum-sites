Method

How I diagnose operating failure.

Persistent execution problems usually form under one of three conditions.

Truth
Whether narrative aligns with evidence.

When the people making decisions cannot see the same reality, organizations begin solving the wrong problem.

Authority
Whether decision rights match accountability.

When the person held accountable cannot actually decide, you get delay, workarounds, and permanent escalation.

Continuity
Whether decisions, rationale, and learning persist over time.

When organizations lose memory, the same issues return under new names.
