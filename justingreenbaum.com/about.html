Professional Bio

Justin Greenbaum is a customer operations and experience executive who leads and transforms complex customer operations. Over 20 years at Comcast, he advanced from frontline technical support to Vice President, leading customer care, technical support, escalations, digital service, regulatory response, customer intelligence and global partner operations. That experience informs how he connects customer needs with the people, systems, and decisions required to serve them.

His leadership responsibilities included up to 1,300 employees, thousands of partner agents and an operating budget in the $200 million range. He led Xfinity Mobile care across sales, service, retention, digital and social channels, scaling partner operations from 50 to 5,000 agents in one year. He also expanded brand-owned communities and the Xfinity support subreddit to help customers find answers and resolve issues without assisted contact, while giving Product, Sales and Retention teams the feedback to improve products, support growth and address churn.

Justin has led enterprise technology work from procurement through implementation, adoption and vendor renewal. At Comcast he led the implementation and expansion of Sprinklr in partnership with Product, Procurement, Legal and Finance. His experience also includes privacy and regulatory operations, working closely with Legal and Compliance on sensitive customer matters.

His leadership emphasizes trust, candid feedback and clear accountability. During a major consolidation, his teams held the highest employee Net Promoter Scores among Comcast frontline teams, with 15 consecutive employee surveys above 90% participation.

In 2026 he founded Greenbaum Labs, an independent advisory and applied-AI practice focused on organizational diagnostics, operating-model design and enterprise AI adoption. He developed Coherence, a diagnostic framework and toolset for examining information gaps, unclear decision ownership and breakdowns in follow-through, and builds tools for analyzing customer feedback and how brands appear in AI-generated answers.

Justin earned an Executive Certificate in Digital Business from MIT Sloan Executive Education and completed its AI Executive Academy.
